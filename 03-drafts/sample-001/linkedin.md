# SAMPLE / 示例: Give every draft a clear next action

A fictional editorial team reaches its weekly review with several drafts and no shared definition of what comes next. A useful starting point is to write three answers beside each draft: the intended reader, the practical takeaway, and the owner of the next edit.

The same topic can then become different versions. A longer article can explain the reasoning. A short post can focus on one step. A community discussion can ask readers to compare their own practice.

This checklist does not establish performance gains. It simply makes the next decision visible. A human still checks claims, approves the final copy, and publishes it manually.
