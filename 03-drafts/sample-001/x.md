SAMPLE / 示例: A fictional weekly content review checklist. Start with one reader and one problem, then give each draft a concrete next step.
---
Ask: Who is this for? What should the reader be able to try? Who owns the next edit? Keep the answers next to the draft so the review has a shared starting point.
---
Adapt the delivery: explain context in an article, focus a thread on a sequence, and invite experience in a community discussion. Have a human check claims and approve the final text.
---
Try the three questions in your next review. Which question would you add? This is an illustrative workflow, with no measured outcome claims.
