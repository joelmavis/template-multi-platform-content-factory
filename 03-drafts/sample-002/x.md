SAMPLE / 示例: A handoff note that survives a pause. A fictional editorial scenario, with no measured outcome claims.
---
A paused draft needs more than a label. Write down the last decision before asking another editor to continue.
---
Name the unresolved question. “Check the second paragraph for an unsupported claim” gives the next editor a place to start.
---
Keep the owner and next date visible. An unassigned task is still open, even when its wording looks finished.
---
Separate a working suggestion from a decision. A question in a comment is not an approval.
---
Before resuming, check whether the intended reader and practical takeaway still fit. Old context can require a fresh discussion.
---
What context would you leave for the next editor? A human must review any real adaptation before manual publication.
