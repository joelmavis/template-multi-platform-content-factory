# SAMPLE / 示例: A handoff note that survives a pause

This fictional editorial scenario concerns a handoff note. It describes no real team and offers no measured results. An editor opens an older draft and finds a date, a title, and several unresolved comments. Those items show that work happened, but they do not explain what the next contributor should do.

A team discussion can start with one question: what decision would let the next contributor continue? Record that decision with a responsible role and a date. If nobody has accepted the task, leave ownership visibly unassigned and bring it to planning.

Before any real posting, a human should check destination rules and review the wording. This SAMPLE remains an illustrative adaptation, not publication authorization.
