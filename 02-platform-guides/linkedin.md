# linkedin editorial guide

Public template. All examples are fictional.

- Language: English.
- Length: 80–400 words of body text, including title and sample label. These are local editorial defaults.
- Formatting and title/hook: First two lines state a professional problem; short paragraphs.
- Tone: Professional, specific, and modest.
- CTA and hashtags: Ask one relevant question; 0–3 relevant hashtags.
- Links: Use a verified supporting link only if it helps the reader.
- Image planning: 1200 × 1200, 1:1; use generous safe margins. Confirm upload/crop requirements in the destination editor; provide alt text and usage rights.

The validator uses `platform-rules.json`; it counts Unicode code points for characters and whitespace-delimited words. For X, every nonempty block separated by a standalone `---` line is one tweet, including its label and numbering. Do not place front matter in the body. Human review must confirm language quality, accessibility, accuracy, and audience fit.
