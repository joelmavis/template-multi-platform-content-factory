# medium editorial guide

Public template. All examples are fictional.

- Language: English.
- Length: 300–2000 words of body text, including title and sample label. These are local editorial defaults.
- Formatting and title/hook: Descriptive title, clear opening, subheads, and a useful conclusion.
- Tone: Explanatory and evidence-aware.
- CTA and hashtags: Invite a concrete experiment; use 0–5 relevant topic tags in the publishing editor.
- Links: Link evidence near the claim; disclose relevant relationships.
- Image planning: 1600 × 900, 16:9; add alt text and credit. Confirm upload/crop requirements in the destination editor; provide alt text and usage rights.

The validator uses `platform-rules.json`; it counts Unicode code points for characters and whitespace-delimited words. For X, every nonempty block separated by a standalone `---` line is one tweet, including its label and numbering. Do not place front matter in the body. Human review must confirm language quality, accessibility, accuracy, and audience fit.
