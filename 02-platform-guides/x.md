# x editorial guide

Public template. All examples are fictional.

- Language: English.
- Length: 100–2500 chars of body text, including title and sample label. These are local editorial defaults.
- Formatting and title/hook: A specific first-tweet hook; separate tweets with a line containing --- .
- Tone: Direct and conversational; each tweet adds a point.
- CTA and hashtags: One reply invitation in the final tweet; 0–2 relevant hashtags.
- Links: Include only necessary verified links; this validator conservatively counts literal URL characters.
- Image planning: 1600 × 900, 16:9; keep text readable on mobile. Confirm upload/crop requirements in the destination editor; provide alt text and usage rights.

The validator uses `platform-rules.json`; it counts Unicode code points for characters and whitespace-delimited words. For X, every nonempty block separated by a standalone `---` line is one tweet, including its label and numbering. Do not place front matter in the body. Human review must confirm language quality, accessibility, accuracy, and audience fit.
