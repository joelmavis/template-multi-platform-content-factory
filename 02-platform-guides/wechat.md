# wechat editorial guide

Public template. All examples are fictional.

- Language: Simplified Chinese.
- Length: 180–5000 chars of body text, including title and sample label. These are local editorial defaults.
- Formatting and title/hook: A clear Chinese title; short paragraphs and descriptive subheads.
- Tone: Warm, useful, restrained.
- CTA and hashtags: End with one practical invitation; 0–2 relevant topic tags.
- Links: Use a descriptive source link only when verified; preview inside the editor.
- Image planning: 900 × 383, about 2.35:1; keep essential text centered. Confirm upload/crop requirements in the destination editor; provide alt text and usage rights.

The validator uses `platform-rules.json`; it counts Unicode code points for characters and whitespace-delimited words. For X, every nonempty block separated by a standalone `---` line is one tweet, including its label and numbering. Do not place front matter in the body. Human review must confirm language quality, accessibility, accuracy, and audience fit.
